Privacy Policy

Effective September 4, 2026 · Developer: Jacob Boyer

Worship Now Studio is church presentation software. Your presentation library is stored on your Mac, with optional synchronization through your private Apple iCloud account. This release does not require a separate Worship Now online account.

No advertising or tracking

Studio does not include advertising, third-party analytics, or tracking across apps and websites. The app does not upload your presentation library to a developer-operated server. Apple may process App Store and optional diagnostic information under its own privacy settings and policies.

Your files and optional iCloud sync

Services, timelines, song edits, scripture content, media, settings, and recovery backups are processed on your device. Studio accesses files you select for importing, saving, exporting, and presenting.

If you enable iCloud Sync, the selected library content is transferred to Apple’s private CloudKit storage associated with your Apple Account so your Macs can synchronize. The developer does not receive that private library. Network access is needed for synchronization and for downloading cloud-only media; downloaded content can be used locally.

Local copies, exported files, recovery backups, and cloud copies may remain until you remove them. Turning sync off does not erase existing copies. You can remove content in the app, manage local files and backups on your Mac, and manage iCloud storage through Apple’s settings. Contact support if you need guidance removing remaining library or backup data. Uninstalling the app alone may not remove those copies.

Remote connections and permissions

When you enable Remote, Studio communicates with connected Worship Now Remote devices on your local network. Slide content, previews, media playback state, commands, and connection information are exchanged so the Remote can display and control the service. This communication is not sent to a developer-operated server.

Use a trusted network and share access codes only with intended operators. You can stop Remote in Studio or revoke Local Network permission in device settings. Studio may also request access to user-selected files and Screen Recording for its optional recording workflow. Permissions are used for the feature you choose and can be managed in macOS System Settings.

Apple purchases

Apple processes purchases and subscriptions. Studio uses StoreKit transaction and entitlement information to verify access, restore purchases, and show subscription status. The app does not receive your payment-card details or Apple Account password. Previously verified access information may be stored locally for offline use.

Apple’s handling of purchase, iCloud, and diagnostic information is described in Apple’s privacy policy. Subscriptions are governed by the Apple Standard End User License Agreement and the purchase terms shown by Apple.

When you contact support

If you email support, the developer receives your email address and whatever message, diagnostic details, or attachments you choose to send. These are used to respond to your request and troubleshoot the issue, not for advertising. Please remove passwords, Remote codes, payment details, and private church information before sending files.

Support correspondence is retained as needed to address the request, maintain related support records, and meet applicable obligations. You can request deletion of support correspondence by emailing the contact below; information that must be retained for legal or security reasons may be excepted.

This website and external services

This informational website has no advertising, analytics scripts, or contact-form database. It is hosted on GitHub Pages. GitHub may process visitor technical information to operate and secure hosting; see GitHub’s privacy statement. Following a link to Apple, using your email provider, or broadcasting through OBS and a streaming platform is governed by those services’ own policies.

Changes and questions

This page will be updated if the app’s data practices change, with a revised effective date.

For privacy questions or deletion requests concerning information sent to support, contact Jacob Boyer at wehonorgod2@gmail.com.
