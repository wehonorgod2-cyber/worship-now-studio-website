How can we help?

Help for service planning, presentation, and the Worship Now Remote connection. Studio requires macOS 14 or later.

Contact support

Email Worship Now Studio at worshipnowstudio@gmail.com.

Include your app version, macOS version, what you expected, and what happened. A screenshot can help. Please do not send passwords, Remote access codes, payment details, or private church information.

Subscriptions and purchases

Purchases are handled by Apple. In Studio, open Settings → Plan to use Manage Subscription or Restore Purchases. Restore with the Apple Account that made the purchase. Eligible previous customers can use Existing Customer Access on the welcome screen.

A free introductory trial is available only when Apple confirms eligibility. After the offer ends, the chosen subscription renews automatically unless canceled. See Apple’s subscription cancellation instructions or request a refund from Apple.

iCloud synchronization

Use the same Apple Account on your Macs and enable iCloud for Worship Now. Open Settings → iCloud Sync. On first sync, choose Use iCloud Library or Upload This Mac only after checking which copy you want to keep; Cancel leaves the choice unfinished.

Edit on one Mac at a time. Sync that Mac before continuing on another. If Studio reports conflicting changes, keep the correct library rather than repeatedly retrying. The app creates recovery backups before replacement. Disabling sync does not delete your iCloud copy or existing backups.

Before a service, make sure its media is downloaded locally and briefly preview it. iCloud-only files require a connection before they are available for offline presentation.

Connect Worship Now Remote

Keep your Mac and iPhone or iPad on the same trusted local network. Start Remote in Studio, allow Local Network access when requested, then connect using the displayed address and the appropriate access code. Guest access can have fewer controls than owner access.

If discovery fails, check Wi-Fi, Local Network permission, firewall settings, and whether the network isolates devices. Reconnect from the Remote’s connection panel. Keep access codes private.

Displays and livestream output

Enable Outputs, select a destination, and assign its physical display before starting it. An enabled but unassigned destination will not appear on a monitor.

For broadcasting from one Mac display, use the Livestream window-only capture workflow and select the correct Worship Now window in OBS or your capture software. A transparent window can show your desktop when viewed directly; the broadcast result depends on the capture source and compositing settings.

Worship Now’s Livestream destination supplies a visual output. OBS or another broadcasting application is needed to send it to YouTube, Facebook, or another streaming service.

Media playback

Keep imported source media and a backup of your service. If a file is unavailable, locate or reimport the original and allow any iCloud download to complete. For a playback problem, tell support the video format, where it is stored, which outputs are active, and whether Remote is connected.
